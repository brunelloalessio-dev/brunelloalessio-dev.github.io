About Me

I'm a Frontend Tech Lead with over 10 years of experience building modern, performant, and scalable web and mobile applications. I focus on designing intuitive digital solutions, leading development teams, and contributing to architectural decisions to ensure high-quality products.

I'm deeply passionate about artificial intelligence and stay relentlessly up to date: I follow the latest releases, try them immediately, and bring them into real work—beyond the hype, with concrete impact.
