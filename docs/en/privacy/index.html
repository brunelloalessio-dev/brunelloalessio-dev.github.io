Privacy

Updated 24 November 2026

In short

This is my personal portfolio. I don’t use analytics, ads, or profiling cookies, and there is no consent banner because there is nothing to accept.

Controller

Alessio Brunello. To ask what I keep about you, or to have it deleted, write to orecchia.mobiletto.0h@icloud.com.

Visiting the site

Pages are static files published on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives connection data, including the IP address, in order to deliver the page. I don’t read those logs and I don’t use them to recognize you.

The typeface and icons are served from this same domain. The browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other CDN.

On your device

Light or dark theme is stored in localStorage. A sessionStorage flag remembers whether the intro animation already played in this tab. They stay in the browser and don’t identify you. You can clear them from the browser’s site settings.

If you email me

There is no form. The message goes from your mail app to my iCloud inbox. I use it only to reply. I don’t pass it on and I don’t add you to a newsletter. I keep it while the conversation is relevant, and I delete it if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, or deletion of the messages you sent me. You can also contact the Italian data protection authority: www.garanteprivacy.it.
